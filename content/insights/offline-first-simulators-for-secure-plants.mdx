## The Air-Gapped Reality of Energy Facilities

Modern cloud-native software architectures rely heavily on persistent internet connectivity, microservices hosted on public hyperscalers, and continuous telemetry pipelines. 

In refineries, LNG liquefaction terminals, and petrochemical facilities, however, the IT/OT boundary is protected by strict ISA/IEC 62443 standards and national cybersecurity mandates. The Industrial Control Systems (ICS) and control room environments are air-gapped from the public internet.

Simulators that require persistent cloud handshakes, remote licensing tokens, or external API dependencies are immediately disqualified by plant CISO and OT security teams.

## The Architecture of Offline Autonomy

Nexiiva's simulation engine is engineered for full standalone execution:
- **Zero Cloud Runtime Dependence**: Once deployed, the process simulation engine, DCS graphics engine, and instructor fault console run 100% locally on standard industrial PCs or ruggedized laptops.
- **Local Thermodynamic Solver**: Numerical integration of multi-stage distillation columns and heat exchanger trains occurs in real time without external API calls.
- **Local Cryptographic Scoring**: Trainee assessment logs, timestamped action audits, and printable performance certificates are generated and stored locally in tamper-evident formats.
- **Portability for Plant Turnarounds**: Training units can be operated inside control room training annexes, portable site trailers, or off-site corporate training academies with equal fidelity.
